# Form & Frame: bespoke joinery and kitchen search plan

Reviewed 5 October 2026. Website batch B17, `b17-local-bespoke-seo`.

## The commercial priority

Position Form & Frame as a Luton-based provider of bespoke joinery, fitted furniture and individually specified kitchens, while retaining the independent kitchen-fitting service. The portfolio supports much more than installation alone: wardrobes, dressing rooms, alcove cupboards, libraries, media walls, home offices and detailed individual furniture.

The strongest starting targets are searches that name both a service and a relevant location. “Luxury” and “handmade” can qualify an enquiry, but they do not replace the furniture type people are looking for. The recommendations below are an editorial assessment of commercial intent, service fit and current search-result examples. They are **not measured search volumes, CPCs or guaranteed rankings**.

Luton is the core location. ## What “most expensive keywords” can and cannot tell us

Actual advertising costs require a Google Ads Keyword Planner report using the relevant geography and date range. No authenticated Keyword Planner or Search Console dataset was available for this audit. No numerical volume, bid or difficulty claims have been invented.

Export the suggested themes with location filters for the actual service area, English language and Google search. Capture average monthly searches, competition, top-of-page bid low/high ranges, currency, date range and location settings. Compare the last 12 months and the monthly breakdown. Very local phrases may have low or grouped volume, so compare both town-specific and broader service phrases. [Google explains Keyword Planner statistics and bid ranges](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en-2).

An expensive paid click is not proof of a good organic target. Prioritise the services with a strong portfolio, good project value, capacity to deliver and a realistic enquiry-to-sale rate. Complete bespoke kitchens and dressing-room schemes are plausible high-value enquiries for this business; that is a commercial judgement, not a claim about their CPC.

## Materials: what the photos establish

The owner reports that veneer, often on MDF, accounts for much of the work. That is useful business context, but it does not identify the substrate or species of each cabinet. Public descriptions therefore distinguish visible grain, reflective finishes, mirrors and metallic details from an unverified construction specification.

Clear reflective or high-gloss examples include **G11, G16, G36, G38, G39, G70 and G72**, with glossy cupboard fronts also visible in **G35 and G77**. These are descriptions of appearance, not confirmation that every surface is high-gloss paint. Useful metal-detail examples include **G08, G11, G19, G44, G64 and G69**. Source-established oak/walnut, leather and brass descriptions are retained where supported by the earlier source audit; ambiguous metal colour is described as metallic or brass-toned.

The next evidence improvement is a project specification register: substrate, veneer species, coating, sheen, metal and hardware supplier for each confirmed item. Existing samples, drawings or invoices can establish those details. Do not infer solid timber, a coating chemistry or an exact alloy from a photograph.

The guide's distinction between real wood veneer and its supporting panel is consistent with [Finsa's explanation of natural veneer for kitchen furniture](https://www.finsa.com/en/w/natural-textured-surfaces-for-kitchen-furniture); Finsa also documents [MDF combined with real wood veneer](https://www.finsa.com/documents/20121/ded4801b-1143-73d6-45b8-1b08efc8a153?download=true). These references explain materials generally and do not establish which products were used in a Form & Frame project.

## Next actions, in order

### Now: establish visibility and a measurable baseline

1. **Google Business Profile:** complete and verify it, confirm the most accurate primary and secondary categories, services, contact details, genuine service area and current photographs. Represent a service-area business accurately; do not create unstaffed local branches. Local results depend substantially on relevance, distance and prominence. [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).
2. **Search Console:** confirm ownership of the correct domain property, submit `https://formandframekitchens.co.uk/sitemap.xml`, inspect the homepage and priority service pages, and check page indexing. Record the baseline for clicks, impressions, queries, pages, country and device. The absence of a verification tag in the repository does not establish whether DNS verification already exists. [Search Console setup guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start).
3. **Request recrawling selectively:** inspect the changed homepage and key services after publication. The sitemap supports discovery of the wider gallery. Repeated requests do not create a guaranteed priority. [Google's recrawl guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/ask-google-to-recrawl).
4. **Measure enquiries:** decide on a suitable analytics and consent setup. Track successful form completion and phone/WhatsApp clicks without sending names, email addresses, messages, plans or private gallery information to analytics. Check real delivery of an owner-authorised test enquiry separately.

### Next 30 days: strengthen trust and the evidence behind the work

5. Request genuine reviews from completed clients and keep the profile photographs current. No incentives, fabricated reviews or review gating. Link relevant work and feedback with the client's permission.
6. Confirm the manufacturing description and project material specifications. Add project scope, design contribution, installation challenges and outcomes where the owner can verify them. Where consent allows, add a real owner portrait and fuller professional background, plus an honest explanation of design, manufacture and installation responsibilities.
7. Obtain the Keyword Planner export and a Search Console query export. Replace the provisional priority order with observed demand and qualified enquiry value. Do not choose phrases solely because a bid estimate is high.
8. Review mobile Core Web Vitals using real-user data when available and a repeatable lab test. Check LCP images, image dimensions, caching and layout movement. B17 keeps existing encodings and avoids adding a third-party tracking script; browser layout checks are not a substitute for a measured performance baseline.

### Days 30–90: improve from evidence

9. Compare rolling 28-day Search Console periods, separating branded and non-branded searches. Look for relevant queries gaining impressions but few clicks, useful pages that are not indexed, and service enquiries that become suitable projects.
10. Add genuinely distinct local case studies as suitable work is completed. A future Harpenden or St Albans page should contain useful local evidence and service detail, rather than a swapped town name. Earn relevant links through genuine designer, builder, supplier and professional relationships; avoid purchased link schemes and bulk directory submissions. [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
11. Develop cost/process information from real quoting data: what affects a wardrobe price, what clients need before a survey, and typical stages of a bespoke kitchen. Publish price bands or timescales only when the business can support them.
12. If immediate enquiries are needed, evaluate a separate, capped search-ad test after conversion measurement and a budget are approved. This audit does not launch advertising or authorise spend.

## How to judge progress

“Top five percent of websites” is not a defined Google target. Use a fixed set of commercially relevant service/location queries, measured consistently, together with non-branded impressions, qualified enquiries and won work. Top-five positions can be an aspiration for that defined set, not a promise across all locations and users.

Recrawling and indexing can take time, and meaningful search-performance changes may take weeks or months. Review initial indexing after publication and assess trends over repeated 28-day periods; 8–12 weeks is a useful first review window, not a ranking deadline. [Google on evaluating search changes](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops).

Longer copy helps only when it answers a real question or explains the work. Google has no preferred word count. The galleries now contain more specific information, without filling every paragraph with locations or unsupported luxury claims. [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
