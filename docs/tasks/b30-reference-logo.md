The wardrobe geometry is reconstructed from measured reference positions: fine top/bottom rails, a fine central seam, separate mirrored Fs with tapered verticals and arms, and two gold handles. There is no enclosing heavy box. The wordmark and subtitle are vector outlines traced directly from the light JPEG, with small contour simplification to remove pixel stair-stepping. They do not use Georgia or another replacement font. The original font identity and an original designer-supplied vector file are not claimed. The ampersand retains the supplied upright outline and the same colour as the other letters.

Flat gold tones are sampled from the supplied references: light handles `#bd8d52`, light divider `#b1905a`, dark handles `#c5984f`, dark divider `#be9c4e`. This preserves their warm tonal difference without copying JPEG compression or lighting variation. The site's general brass token is unchanged.
