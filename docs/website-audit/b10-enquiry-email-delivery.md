# B10 enquiry email delivery

## Purpose

B10 replaces the placeholder generic enquiry-provider boundary with direct transactional email delivery through Resend.

- `RESEND_API_KEY`

## Delivery behaviour

1. The website validates all required fields on the server.
2. The website validates PDF/JPEG/PNG attachment signatures and limits.
3. The business notification is sent first to the Form & Frame inbox.
4. The business email includes every relevant completed form field and the enquiry reference.
5. Uploaded files are attached to the business notification.
6. The customer email address is used as Reply-To on the business notification when present.
7. Only after the business notification succeeds does the website accept the enquiry.
8. If the customer supplied an email address, a branded acknowledgement is sent automatically.
9. The acknowledgement includes the enquiry reference, Arnas Vazinskas, the business telephone number and an inline Form & Frame Kitchens logo.
10. Acknowledgement failure does not cause a duplicate lead: the accepted business notification remains successful and the failure is logged for investigation.
11. The submission ID is used for Resend idempotency keys to reduce accidental duplicate delivery.

## Attachment limits

The form allows PDF, JPEG and PNG files.

Because Vercel Functions enforce a request-body limit for server-routed uploads, B10 uses:
- up to 5 files,
- maximum 3 MB per file,
- maximum 4 MB total.

This leaves payload headroom for multipart form data while preserving direct attachment delivery.

## Verification gate

Do not merge B10 until:
- Resend domain status is verified;
- a sending-only API key is configured in the B10 Vercel preview;
- kitchen, door and bespoke-joinery form variants have been submitted;
- at least one valid PDF or image attachment is confirmed in the received business email;
- the customer acknowledgement is received and visually checked;
- the Form & Frame business notification is present in `sales@formandframekitchens.co.uk`;
- Resend delivery status and Vercel runtime logs show successful delivery;
- invalid/missing-field and invalid-file tests still reject correctly.
